Basin previews

Prototype builds of Basin, a falling-piece terrain and water puzzle.

  • Preview 06 — graphics round 2: turf grass, stone strata, rocky underside, shaded clouds, waterfalls, faster title.
  • Preview 05 — graphics work in progress: water shader, textured terrain, cloud sea, lighting, new clouds and effects.
  • Preview 04 — HUD rebuilt: island fills the screen, runoff dial, compact piece tiles, drawn icons, full-screen button.
  • Preview 03 — island journey, cloud chains, touch-first controls (lift to drop).
  • Preview 02 — earned levels, slower fuse, falling rocks, clearer progress.
  • Preview 01 — first playable: pieces, bloom, halo, water cycle, effects, sound.
